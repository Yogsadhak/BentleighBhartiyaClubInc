Who we are

A community built on warmth & belonging

The Bentleigh Bhartiya Club is a government-recognised, registered non-profit seniors' organisation based in Melbourne. Since June 2023, members have been gathering regularly in the Bentleigh and McKinnon areas, sharing culture, laughter, and support.

Our weekly gatherings feature a rich variety of social, cultural, and health-focused activities — all designed for the senior community to flourish together.

  • 💬 A platform to exchange ideas and life experiences
  • 🤝 Reducing loneliness and homesickness for seniors living abroad
  • 🧠 Encouraging mental and physical activity in later years
  • ✨ Helping members live in Australia with confidence, dignity, and joy
